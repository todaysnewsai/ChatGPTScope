## Contact

For editorial questions, corrections, source information, partnerships, and general enquiries, contact the editorial team through the contact address associated with this publication.

## Corrections and factual issues

Accuracy is our highest priority. If you identify an incorrect fact, outdated capability, broken source, or misleading statement, please include the article title, the specific passage, and a reliable source that supports the correction.

## Press and company communications

Companies and research organizations may submit announcements, documentation, research papers, product information, and media materials for consideration. Submission does not guarantee publication.

## Guest contributions

We may consider original analysis and technical contributions that provide genuine value to readers. Promotional copy, copied material, undisclosed AI-generated submissions, and content designed primarily for search manipulation are not accepted.

## Response policy

We cannot guarantee a response to every message, but substantive correction requests receive priority.
