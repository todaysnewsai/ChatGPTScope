AI Tech Articles is an independent English-language publication focused on artificial intelligence and the technology changing how people work, build software, search for information, and conduct research.

## What we cover

We cover AI models, agents, tools, research, coding, AI search, robotics, enterprise AI, safety, regulation, and the companies building the technology. Our goal is practical reporting: explain what changed, why it matters, what is confirmed, and what readers should watch next.

## Editorial approach

We separate confirmed announcements from company claims, independent reporting, research findings, and analysis. We do not invent benchmarks, prices, quotations, release dates, or product capabilities. When a story is developing, we identify uncertainty rather than presenting speculation as fact.

## Our audience

AI Tech Articles is written for developers, technology professionals, researchers, business readers, students, and curious people who want useful explanations without unnecessary jargon.

## Corrections

If you find an error, please contact us with the article URL and the relevant claim. We review factual corrections carefully and update published material when warranted.
