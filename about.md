**Articles About AI** is an independent technology publication that helps readers understand the fast-changing world of artificial intelligence. We cover AI products, models, companies, and practical tools—not just one chatbot or provider.

Our coverage includes:

- **AI news:** important product launches, model updates, research announcements, and changes to AI services.
- **Reviews and comparisons:** how tools differ, what they do well, and where their limitations matter.
- **Practical guides:** clear, step-by-step help for using AI tools and understanding their features.
- **AI tools and technology:** explainers about assistants, creative tools, coding products, research systems, and emerging capabilities.

## Our approach

We aim to make technical developments understandable without overstating what a product can do. We distinguish confirmed announcements from speculation, explain when a feature is limited or rolling out gradually, and link to official documentation or primary sources when available.

Our reviews and guides focus on practical usefulness: who a tool may suit, what readers should check before relying on it, and what limitations to keep in mind. AI systems can make mistakes, so important outputs should be verified.
