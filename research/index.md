Research follows advances in machine learning, reasoning, multimodal systems, agents, robotics, AI for science, evaluation, and safety.

## Reading research responsibly

A paper, preprint, benchmark, or demonstration is evidence of a result under particular conditions. It is not automatically proof of broad real-world generalization.

We explain methods and limitations in accessible language while preserving important technical distinctions.
